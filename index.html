What's inside

How it's made

Created with AI and checked in multiple stages: automatic checks (readings, word and kanji level), two independent AI reviews, a back-translation check, and two dedicated Japanese-language reviews (accuracy and politeness; natural, everyday Japanese).

No check is perfect. If you find a mistake, tell us with the item number (for example N5-v-0012). We fix it and send you the updated version for free. Not satisfied? We'll refund you.
